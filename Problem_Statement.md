# Problem Statement

## 1. Title
ZestHub - Restaurant Discovery and Review Platform

## 2. Domain
Food Technology

## 3. Who is the User?
- Customer
- Restaurant Owner
- Admin

## 4. What Problem Are We Solving?
People often struggle to discover restaurants based on cuisine, ratings, and location. Restaurant owners also need a platform to showcase their businesses. ZestHub provides a centralized platform to discover restaurants, read reviews, and explore menus.

## 5. Proposed Solution
- User Registration & Login
- Restaurant Listing
- Restaurant Details
- Reviews & Ratings
- Search & Filter
- Favorite Restaurants

## 6. Core Database Tables
- Users
- Restaurants
- Categories
- Reviews
- Favorites
- MenuItems

## 7. User Roles
- Admin
- Customer
- Restaurant Owner

## 8. Success Criteria
Users should be able to discover a restaurant in less than one minute.

## 9. Out of Scope
- Online Food Delivery
- Live Order Tracking
- Payment Gateway

## 10. Chosen Track
Python (FastAPI)
